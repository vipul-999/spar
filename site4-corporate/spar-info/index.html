About Us

For over three decades, we have been a trusted leader in designing and executing Geotechnical projects pan India. With excellent expertise in Slope Stabilization, Rockfall Mitigation, Ground Improvement, Foundation Engineering, River Protection Works, Reinforced Earth Walls, and Road Construction, our commitment to cater comprehensive geoengineering services stands as a pioneer bar none.

Driven by a vision of nationwide infrastructural development; our proficiency and vast experience have facilitated our overall establishment in this industry and led us to the forefront of Geotechnical and Infrastructure services. Our unparalleled dedication and persistent perspective have positioned us as the focus Key Opinion Leader in offering our solutions in the geoengineering field. By providing safety through our work, we aim to secure the nation and address all advancements that help build the nation. We also ensure that our top-tier services and innovative solutions facilitate the nation’s progress in the best possible way while keeping sustainability in check.

With a mission to deliver utmost safety in the most demanding environments, we adapt and evolve with the changing times to offer dynamic yet strengthening solutions in different situations. Our experienced team has the forte in contributing consistent developments in all kinds of Geotechnical projects throughout the country. Our global associations, along with our sincere commitment in defining quality in our projects, set us apart and make us the preferred choice for our clients.

By upholding our dedication and pursuit of excellence, we continue to build a safer and more resilient India, one project at a time.

VISION

To lead the way in building a resilient and safer India by pioneering geotechnical and infrastructural advancements that foster sustainability and nationwide development.

MISSION

To deliver comprehensive geotechnical solutions that prioritize safety, quality, and innovation, addressing the toughest environmental challenges to secure India’s infrastructure and support the nation’s progress.

Our Values

  • Client Satisfaction

    At Geo Spar, our priority is the satisfaction of our client. We make sure that our client is part of our entire work process and is satisfied with our work from the beginning to the end.

  • Team

    Our Team is the backbone of our company and plays an immense role in our success. We understand the value of our people and focus on increasing our team strength by hiring highly skilled and knowledgeable professionals.

  • Quality

    All Geo Spar projects have one thing in common, which is our commitment to the quality of work. We are focused on delivering well-executed projects with the best quality work within a planned time frame. We believe that our quality work is the foundation of our success.

  • Adaptability

    As a company, we adapt ourselves as per our client requirements, project, and location. We make sure to change even the minutest of the details wherever required, and that has been our forte for executing different services with the same perfection.

  • Integrity

    Geo Spar has been in business for more than a decade. Our integrity has been an important factor for our clients to trust us with their work requirements. It has also been a reason for us to be establishing ourselves as a specialized geoengineering solution provider in India.

ACCOMPLISHMENTS

  • We have received multiple accolades for being the first company in India to construct the substructure and foundation of the highest multi-span bridge — the Col. B Santosh Babu Bridge — using the innovative Tam Grouted Micropile technology at an elevation of 14,900 ft above mean sea level (MSL).
  • Till date, we have worked upon nearly 3,00,000 Sq. Mtr. (Three lakh sq. mtr) area of slope stabilization works.
  • We are also the first company in India to execute 3100-kN double corrosion protection ground anchors at Rohtang Tunnel Project,
    Himachal Pradesh.
